EVE Spai.

Intel, maps and wormholes for EVE Online, built by and for the Imperium.

It reads your intel channels as reports land, puts hostiles on the map, keeps your wormhole chains, looks up a local in one paste, turns nearby kills into battle reports and answers your questions by voice. Free and open source.

What it does

Most intel, map, wormhole, lookup and battle features work for any capsuleer. Jabber pings are made for the Imperium's own ping format.

01

Live intel

EVE Spai reads the intel channels you pick as reports land and turns each line into a card: systems, pilots, hulls and gang size worked out, and the jumps from you counted over gates and your jump bridges.

  • Alerts you design: by distance, count, hulls, keywords, channel or tagged pilots.
  • Heard and seen: a sound per rule, desktop notifications, an alert window over the game and phone push.
  • Knows what it read: clears, kills, cynos, bubbles and "no visual" are picked out.
02

Star map and routes

An interactive map of the universe or one region, with hostiles lit as they are reported and the layers you want: sovereignty, activity, jump bridges with their Ansiblex zones, gate camps, Jove observatories and wormhole links.

  • Route planner: gate, capital jump and titan bridge routes, with systems to avoid and warnings on the way.
  • Straight to your client: set the route as waypoints in the game with one click.
  • Holes included: routes can go through the wormholes you know, filtered by mass, time and size.
03

Wormholes

Every chain you know on one map, each hole coloured by the time it has left and drawn by the mass it has left. Pinned systems show how many jumps they are from each chain.

  • Signatures: paste a probe scan and every signature is kept per system, searchable in one browser.
  • Found for you: holes your characters jump through are recorded, Thera and Turnur come from EVE-Scout.
  • Shared safely: share chains with your group, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Scan routes: send one or more scouts around your space, nearest systems first.
04

Lookup

Paste local and get every pilot in one table: danger, kills and losses, gang size, where and how they fly, standings and your own notes.

  • Habits at a glance: tags for FC, cyno, black ops, logi, bait and more.
  • Your call: hide blues, pick the columns, keep a history of lookups.
05

Battle reports

Kills near you cluster into battles on their own: sides, efficiency, losses and hulls worked out. Split an engagement, move a group to the other side or scrub a stray kill, then share it as a link on eve-spai.com.

06

Jabber, made for the Imperium

Fleet pings and chat without leaving the app. Pings are read into FC, formup, doctrine and comms, with a button to join Mumble.

  • Rooms and DMs: in the app or in their own windows.
  • Ping rules: pick which pings sound, pop up or reach your phone.
  • Over the game: a ping window shows new fleets while you play.
07

Assistant

Ask about the situation in plain words, typed or spoken: where a gang went, what is near you, the fastest safe route home. It reads the same intel, kills, maps and pings you see and answers in a few lines, with systems, ships and killmails linked.

  • Talk to it: push to talk while you play and hear the answer; speech can run on your own machine.
  • Your model: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, a local model through Ollama or LM Studio, or your Claude or ChatGPT subscription.
  • You decide what it reads: tick what it may see; Jabber and intel only ever go to the model you picked.
  • Watches: ask it to keep an eye on a gang or a system and it speaks up when something matches.
  • Asks before it acts: map highlights, routes, Jabber messages and fleet actions wait for your yes.

Make it yours

Nearly everything can be set to how you fly. A few of the things you can change:

  • Alert rulesTop rule wins: match on severity, distance, count, hulls, channels, characters and tags; notify, pop up, push or stay quiet.
  • Ping rulesPer FC, doctrine, PAP type or keyword: a sound, a notification, a push, or nothing.
  • SoundsA sound and a volume for each kind of event, one master volume and mute.
  • ThemesCaldari, Amarr, Minmatar, Gallente and Daylight, or your own three colours.
  • Map layersSovereignty by alliance or coalition, activity, jump bridges, zones, camps and more.
  • Jump bridges and zonesPaste your network, set the capital and the Ansiblex zone routes may use.
  • Intel channelsPick yours or start from a coalition pack; turn characters off one by one.
  • LanguageSix languages to start with, asked for when you set up and changed any time.
  • Wormhole routesWhich kinds of hole routes may use, and how much mass, time and size they need.
